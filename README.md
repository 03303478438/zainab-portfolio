# Zainab Soomro - English Language Teaching Internship Portfolio

A comprehensive, interactive web portfolio documenting the English language teaching internship of **Zainab Soomro** at **Government College for Information Technology, Gurunagar, Hyderabad** (September 2026).

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Profile & Introduction:** Highlighting academic focus in Applied Linguistics, role details, and personal statement.
- **Placement & Institutional Details:** Overview of GCIT Hyderabad, supervision under Asst. Prof. Areej Ijlal and Prof. Nawab Ali Rahoo.
- **Internship Objectives:** 11 core pedagogical and linguistic objectives with interactive indicators.
- **Logbook & Hours Breakdown:** Detailed daily activity logs with filterable views (Teaching Days vs. Orientation/Meetings) and visual hours charts.
- **Reflective Journal:** Weekly reflections following experiential learning frameworks.
- **Evidence & Classroom Samples:** 12 photo slots across 6 distinct lessons, featuring teaching photos, student activity photos, and outcome evaluation graphs for every lesson.
- **Supervisor Evaluation & Report Access:** Dedicated sections for institutional verification and report access.
